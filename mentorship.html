Our Commitment

Safeguarding Comes First

Because this ministry works closely with women, mentorship accounts are handled with care, not open-door access.

  • ✓
    Applications reviewed and approved manually — no instant automatic access.
  • ✓
    No private, unmonitored messaging — structured sessions and supervised contact only.
  • ✓
    Clear community guidelines, privacy policy, and a published safeguarding statement.
