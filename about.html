About Us

About For His Purpose

For His Purpose exists to help people know God through His Word and live in faithful response to His call.

In a world where identity is often shaped by culture, achievement, and comparison, we believe lasting transformation begins by knowing Christ. As we grow in the knowledge of God, we become more like Him and are equipped to live the lives He has called us to.

Our desire is to see lives anchored in Scripture, strengthened through prayer, and marked by wholehearted obedience to God. Through Bible studies, biblical teaching, mentorship, worship, prayer, and discipleship, we seek to create spaces where people can encounter God's truth, grow in spiritual maturity, and walk confidently in their God-given purpose.

Whether you are just beginning your walk with Christ or have followed Him for years, our prayer is that you would know Him more deeply, love Him more fully, and faithfully live for His glory.

Because every life finds its greatest meaning when it is lived for His purpose.

About Our Founder

Lolia Dikio

Lolia Dikio is a Bible teacher, worshipper, and the founder of For His Purpose.

She is passionate about helping people know God through His Word, be transformed by its truth, and faithfully live out His purpose for their lives.

Lolia believes that every person has been created by God, on purpose and for His purpose. Yet, in a world that constantly calls us to pursue ourselves, she is convinced that true fulfillment is found in knowing Christ, surrendering to His will, and faithfully living the life He has ordained for us. This conviction is the heartbeat behind For His Purpose.

Through the faithful teaching of Scripture, prayer, worship, and authentic community, Lolia seeks to encourage believers to become deeply rooted in God's Word and boldly walk in alignment with His purpose for their lives. She is especially passionate about seeing women transformed by God's truth and confidently embrace all that He has called them to be.

Her prayer is that every person who encounters For His Purpose would grow in their love for God's Word, hunger for His presence, and faithfully live the life God created them to live — for His glory and His purpose.
