About Us

Our Story, Mission & Kingdom Mandate

For His Purpose was born from a conviction: that every woman and girl carries a purpose too weighty to leave undiscovered.

Meet Our Founder

A vision to raise a generation that lives for God's kingdom.

For His Purpose was founded out of a deep conviction that identity and purpose are not things we invent — they are things God reveals. What began as a personal walk of faith grew into a movement calling women, girls, and anyone hungry for purpose into deeper relationship with Christ.

Our Mission

Establishing God's Kingdom on Earth

To raise people who understand their identity, discover their purpose, grow in character, and live as positive examples in their generation. The movement begins with women and the girl child, but remains open to everyone who desires to grow in faith and purpose.

Our Vision

The Purpose Generation

A generation of women, girls, and believers who know who they are in Christ, walk boldly in their calling, and lead with faith, character, and love in their homes, workplaces, and communities.
