Who We Are

Rooted in Christ, Walking in Purpose

✦
01 · Our Calling

For His Purpose is a Christ-centred community of women committed to growing in faith, deepening their relationship with God, and living intentionally according to His plans and purposes for their lives.

02 · Our Mission

We exist to create a community where women are grounded in the Word, strengthened through prayer, encouraged in sisterhood, and equipped to walk confidently in their God-given purpose.

03 · Our Heart

Our heart is to see women who know God, understand His will, and live lives that bring Him glory.

Our Kingdom Mandate

“To establish God’s kingdom on earth by raising people who understand their identity, discover their purpose, grow in character, and live as positive examples in their generation.”

We believe that when women encounter Christ authentically, their families, communities, and entire spheres of influence are transformed for eternity.

What We Stand On

Our Brand Pillars

Six foundational values that shape everything we do as a community.

✦

Faith

Rooted in Christ and grounded in His Word above all else, trusting God across every season.

♡

Purpose

Helping every woman discover and boldly fulfill what God specifically called and created her to do.

☼

Walking alongside each other with wisdom, discipleship, and intentional spiritual guidance.

♦

A safe, authentic space where sisters grow, pray, worship, and build lifelong bonds together.
