A Welcoming Community

Growing in Faith,
Walking in His Word

This is a welcoming community where women come together to study God's Word, grow in faith, and discover what it means to live purposefully for God. Through Scripture, prayer, fellowship, and meaningful conversations, we seek to deepen our relationship with Christ and align our lives with His purpose.

Whether you are new to the Bible or looking to deepen your understanding of God's Word, you'll find a place to learn, grow, and be encouraged alongside other women on the journey of faith.

Join us as we grow in God's Word and learn to live purposefully for His glory.

How Our Study Works

We meet virtually every Tuesday evening to read Scripture together, share insights, and support each other through prayer.

1
Verse-by-Verse Scripture Study

We explore God's Word with depth and clarity, discussing practical application for modern Christian women.

2
Interactive Fellowship & Questions

A safe, open space to ask questions, share perspectives, and learn from one another in love.

3
Intentional Prayer & Intercession

Every session includes dedicated time to pray for personal requests, families, and each other's walk with God.
