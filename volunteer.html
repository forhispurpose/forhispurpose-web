Join the For His Purpose Team — We’d Love to Have You!

For His Purpose is a Christ-centred community that empowers and supports women to know God deeply, grow in His Word, and live purposefully. Our heart is to raise women who understand their identity in Christ, discover their God-given purpose, and walk confidently in their calling.

We are committed to building a community rooted in the Word of God, prayer, discipleship, fellowship, and service.
