Our Mission

Establishing God's kingdom on earth, one purposeful life at a time.

The movement begins with women and the girl child — but it is not limited to females only. We raise people who understand their identity, discover their purpose, grow in character, and live as positive examples in their generation.

  • ✓
    Faith — rooted in Christ and His Word.
  • ✓
    Purpose — discovering what God made you for.
  • ✓
    Mentorship — guided growth from women who've walked the path.
  • ✓
    Community — fellowship that keeps you accountable and encouraged.
  • ✓
    Leadership — raised to lead in your home, work, and generation.
