Her Calling & Vision

Lolia Dikio

Lolia Dikio is a Bible teacher and the founder of For His Purpose, a Christ-centred community committed to helping people know God deeply, grow in His Word, and faithfully fulfil the purpose for which He created them.

She believes that a life of true fulfilment begins with knowing Christ and surrendering wholeheartedly to His will. In a world that often encourages the pursuit of personal ambition, Lolia is passionate about helping people discover the beauty of a life centred on God, shaped by His Word, and lived for His glory.

Through For His Purpose, she seeks to cultivate a community where the teaching of Scripture, prayer, worship, and fellowship inspire genuine spiritual growth. Her desire is to see believers become firmly rooted in God’s Word, mature in their faith, and walk confidently in obedience to His calling.

She is particularly passionate about seeing women grow in their knowledge of God, become established in their identity in Christ, and embrace the purpose for which He has called them. She believes that knowing God should transform not only what we believe, but also how we live, the choices we make, and the people we become.

Her prayer is that every woman who encounters For His Purpose will grow in her love for Christ, be transformed by His truth, and live a life that brings glory to His name.

“Because every life finds its greatest meaning when it is lived for His purpose.”
